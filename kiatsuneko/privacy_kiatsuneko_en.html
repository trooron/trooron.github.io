Privacy Policy

KiatsuNeko for iOS

KiatsuNeko (the “App”) shows changes in barometric pressure through a little cat. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Location

  • The App uses your location only to show the pressure and weather (including temperature) where you are, and to match the Home screen sky (morning, day, evening, night) to sunset. Sunset times are calculated on your device; your location is not sent anywhere for that.
  • When the App gets pressure and weather data, the coordinates of your location are sent to Apple Weather (Apple’s weather service). The location is stored only on your device to show the latest data, and is not used for any other purpose.
  • If you don’t allow location access, or if you choose a region (a city or a Japanese prefecture) in Settings, the App does not get your current location.
  • If you don’t allow location access and haven’t chosen a region, the App shows the pressure and weather for the main city of your device’s time zone (for example, Chicago or London). The time zone is checked on your device and is not sent anywhere for that.
  • When you search for a city in Settings → Choose region, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps (Apple’s map service) to show suggestions. The name and coordinates of the city you choose are stored only on your device.

2. Mood logs and notes

  • Your mood logs and notes are stored only on your device.
  • They are never sent to any server.
  • If you delete the App, the logs on your device are deleted too.

3. Ads

  • The App shows ads using Google AdMob (an ad service provided by Google LLC).
  • Ads are non-personalized. The App does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not ask for tracking permission.
  • To serve ads, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising identifier and IP address. Your approximate region may be estimated from your IP address when ads are requested.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before showing ads. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Your mood logs and the location the App uses are never used for ads.
  • If you buy the Style Pack (a one-time purchase), ads are not shown.
  • For how Google handles data, see:
    • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
    • Google Privacy Policy

4. Weather data

  • Weather data is provided by Apple Weather (data sources).
  • The App shows estimates based on weather data. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any health condition.

5. Sharing with third parties

Except as described above (coordinates sent to Apple Weather, search text sent to Apple Maps, and ads served by Google AdMob), the App does not share your information with third parties.

6. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland

  • The App has no servers of its own. The developer does not receive your location, mood logs, or other personal information.
  • For ads, we rely on your consent, which you give or refuse in Google’s consent message. You can withdraw or change it anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Information processed by Apple (Apple Weather, Apple Maps) and Google (AdMob) is handled under their own privacy policies.
  • You have rights over your personal information, such as access, correction, deletion, and objection. Data stored by the App stays on your device and can be deleted by deleting the App. For questions, contact us at the address below.
  • You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
