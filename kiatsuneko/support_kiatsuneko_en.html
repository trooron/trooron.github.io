Support

KiatsuNeko for iOS

Thank you for using KiatsuNeko. Here are answers to common questions. If you still need help, feel free to email us at the bottom of this page.

Common questions

I don’t get notifications

  • Check that notifications are allowed in the iPhone Settings app → KiatsuNeko → Notifications.
  • Also check that “Get notifications” is on in the App’s Settings.

It shows the pressure for a city that isn’t where I am

  • If location access isn’t allowed, the App shows the pressure for the main city of your iPhone’s time zone (for example, Chicago). Check the iPhone Settings app → KiatsuNeko → Location.
  • You can also choose a region without using your location: in the App’s Settings, tap Region and search for your city.

What are the “little notes from your cat”?

  • Gentle notes that arrive when you haven’t opened the App in a while.
  • If you don’t want them, turn off “Little notes from your cat” in the App’s Settings.

Do my mood logs sync with my other devices?

  • No. Your logs are stored only on this device and don’t sync with other devices.
  • If you delete the App, the logs on your device are deleted too.

About ads

  • The App shows ads. They are non-personalized, and the App doesn’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, you can change your ad consent anytime in the App’s Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • With the Style Pack (a one-time purchase), ads are not shown.

I bought the Style Pack, but it isn’t unlocked

  • In the App’s Settings, tap Style → “Choose cat & colors,” then tap “Restore purchase.” Please make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
